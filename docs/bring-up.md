# Revision B bring-up and firmware requirements

1. Inspect polarity and IC orientation. Power through a current-limited bench source for initial checks. Record USB input, protected input, board supply, current draw and reference voltages. Check the TPS22918 startup waveform and absence of reverse current at USB-C when the Nano USB port powers the board.
2. Initialize all pot chip-select and DAC chip-select pins inactive before starting SPI. Park unused op-amp channels as shown. Keep unused MCU analog inputs as inputs with pull-ups disabled. Manual switches connect through 10k resistors: firmware output drive can override them; define manual and training modes explicitly.
3. Measure V_LOW, V_HIGH and each V_MID. Check with an oscilloscope for oscillation at reference buffers, U15 pump outputs and U17 ADC buffers, both idle and during worst-case weight/input/DAC steps. The DC behavioral model does not prove stability. Determine a measured settling delay before phase sampling.
4. MCP4251 wiper codes are 0..256 inclusive (257 positions), not an 8-bit 0..255-only scale. W and B are tied; use the nominal mapping in `sim/eqprop/network.py`, then calibrate the actual resistor range. The pots are volatile and require initialization at startup.
5. MCP4822 requires gain=2 for its 0..4.096 V nominal range. The free phase requires **measured V_MID_PUMP**, not automatically 2.500 V or code2048. Use `V_DAC = V_MID_PUMP + I_command / (2e-6 A/V)`. Verify polarity by measuring current at several forced load voltages. Measure free-phase residual current for both channels and calibrate DAC zero and gain.
6. Start with beta=1e-6 A/V, matching the revised training default. Limit commanded current using DAC limits and `V_amp=1.5*(V_DAC+V_load)-2*V_MID_PUMP` with measured supply. Do not use the previous 1M transresistance or unbounded nudge commands. Reduce beta if any equilibrium or command leaves the validated range.
7. Configure ADS1115 single-ended channels for H1,H2,YP,YN as shown. Choose a full-scale range that covers the actual voltages. Allow conversion/channel-switch settling; average after the analog circuit has settled. Calibrate channel offsets. Keep SPI transfers outside equilibrium sampling windows.
8. Verify all four XOR inputs and complementary mux outputs before training. Train, read back taps, power-cycle and restore them, and verify classification with the actual analog hysteresis thresholds. Repeat at intended supply extremes and temperature range before committing to a larger assembly batch.

This is a prototype validation procedure, not a statement that these physical checks have already passed. No firmware source was present in the reviewed workspace, so the interface changes are documented rather than claimed implemented on a microcontroller.

## B.1 learn/stop button

SW3 connects Nano A6 to GND; R_BUTTON1 supplies the external pull-up. Read it as an analog input, not digitalRead or INPUT_PULLUP. Follow the debounce, phase-boundary stop, and assembly requirements in [usability revision](usability-revision.md). This is a firmware requirement, not completed MCU code.

## Startup, sampling and calibration details from pre-commit review

At reset, ignore prediction LEDs until initialization and settling finish. The DAC powers up shut down; shutdown presents about 500 kohm to ground and is **not** the calibrated free phase. Set all CS outputs high before enabling SPI, then program both DAC channels active at their calibrated zero-current codes before accepting samples. The grounded LDAC pin means A and B update sequentially: finish both writes, then settle before measurement. Stop training by restoring active zero-current commands, not by shutting down the DAC. Gain=2 gives nominal 1 mV/code; code 4095 produces 4.095 V, not 4.096 V. Calibrate actual transfer errors. [Microchip MCP4822, sections 4–5](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/20002249B.pdf)

V_MID_PUMP is accessible at TP5 but is not wired to an ADC channel. For initial firmware, enter its DMM-measured value and calibrate both pumps at the intended supply. Repeat calibration after changing the supply source or observing rail drift. A stored fixed DAC zero does not automatically follow the ratiometric midrail; do not claim automatic tracking. Verify residual current over the intended supply variation before unattended demos.

Explicitly select ADS1115 PGA ±4.096 V for the nominal midrail measurements: reset/default ±2.048 V clips them. This setting gives 125 uV/code. Use ±6.144 V if measured inputs can exceed 4.096 V; a wider PGA range does not permit inputs outside GND..VDD. Start a single-shot conversion after configuring each channel and wait for completion before reading it. Hold inputs, weights and both nudge commands constant for the entire four-channel scan; this ADC multiplexes channels rather than sampling simultaneously. Analog settling precedes the conversion. [TI ADS1115, full-scale-range table and configuration register](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1115.pdf)

Keep fractional weight-update accumulators in firmware and quantize the requested conductances when programming taps. Rounding each small learning increment independently may discard it. Characterize this policy in a hardware-in-the-loop training test; the existing continuous-training/final-quantization result does not validate online finite-tap training.
